Wayback Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 3, 2026 (version 1.1: photos added)

Junik Jang ("the developer") publishes the Android app "Wayback" (Korean name: 돌아가는 길, package com.junik.wayback, "the app"). This policy explains what information the app handles.

1. Information the app handles itself

The app has no sign-up or login, and the developer runs no servers for it.

InformationPurposeWhere it stays
Current (precise) locationCalculating distance and direction to your saved places; sunrise and sunset timesProcessed on your device only; never sent anywhere
Places you save (name, note, level, coordinates, time saved)Guiding you back to themStored on your device only
Compass (orientation sensor) readingsPointing the arrowProcessed on your device only
Photos you take (optional)Helping you recognize a saved spotStored on your device only; never sent anywhere

The developer cannot collect or view any of this information. Coordinates leave your device only when you tap "Share location" and choose where to send them.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows one Google AdMob banner ad at the top of the screen. To serve ads, Google may automatically collect:

  • Device identifiers such as the advertising ID
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address
  • Device and app information, ad impressions and clicks, and diagnostics

Google processes this information under its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

To limit personalized ads, delete or reset your advertising ID in your phone's Settings > Google > Ads.

3. Permissions

  • Location (precise/approximate): Used only while the app is open, to find where you are and guide you to saved places. The app never uses location in the background. If you deny it, you can still enter coordinates manually.
  • Internet: Used to load ads.
  • Camera: The app does not request camera permission. When you add a photo, your phone's camera app opens and only the photo you take is passed to the app.

4. Retention and deletion

Saved places and photos are removed from your device when you delete them in the app or uninstall the app. The developer keeps no copy.

5. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or under 14 in Korea), and the developer does not knowingly collect children's personal information.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version and its effective date will be posted on this page.
