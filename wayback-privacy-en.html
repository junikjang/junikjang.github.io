Wayback Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026 (previous version: October 2, 2026)

Junik Jang ("the developer") respects the privacy of people who use the Android app "Wayback" (com.junik.wayback, "the app").

1. Information the app handles itself

The app has no sign-up or login, and the developer runs no servers. Everything below is processed and stored only on your device and is never sent to the developer.

ItemPurposeWhere it is kept
Current location (precise)Distance and direction to saved places, sunrise/sunset times, working out where the car is for the auto parking recordProcessed on the device only
Places you save (name, note, coordinates, level, time)Guiding you backStored on the device only
Photos you take (optional)Helping you recognise a saved placeStored on the device only
Motion sensors (accelerometer, rotation, orientation)Compass direction; estimating steps and walking direction for the auto parking recordProcessed on the device only
Pressure sensorEstimating the level and telling you how many floors up or down a place isProcessed on the device only; saved with the place on the device
Bluetooth device name and type, connect/disconnect timeAuto parking record: noticing when your car’s Bluetooth disconnects (when you get out)Processed on the device only; the list of detected cars is stored on the device

Coordinates leave your device only when you tap “Share location” and pick who to send them to.

2. Auto parking record (only if you turn it on)

  • This feature is off by default and works only after you turn it on in Settings.
  • When your car’s Bluetooth disconnects, the app records steps, direction and air pressure for up to 20 minutes and shows a notification while it does.
  • If you open the app during that time, the app keeps receiving your location for up to 20 minutes even after you close it, to work out where the car is (a foreground service and notification are shown). If you don’t open the app, location is not used.
  • Everything stays on the device. Automatically saved car spots you haven’t confirmed are cleared when you connect to the car again (that is, when you leave).

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows one Google AdMob banner at the top of the screen. To serve ads, Google may automatically collect:

  • Device identifiers such as the advertising ID
  • Approximate location derived from the IP address
  • Device and app information, ad impressions and clicks, diagnostics

This information is handled under Google’s privacy policy.

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from partner apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

To opt out of personalised ads, delete or reset your advertising ID in Settings > Google > Ads.

4. Permissions

  • Location (precise/approximate): used to find where you are and guide you back. The app uses “While using the app” access only and never asks for “Allow all the time” (background location). You can still use the app by typing coordinates if you deny it.
  • Nearby devices (Bluetooth connect): requested only when you turn on the auto parking record, and used only to notice your car’s Bluetooth connecting and disconnecting.
  • Notifications: requested only when you turn on the auto parking record, to show that recording is in progress.
  • Camera: the app does not request camera permission. Adding a photo opens your phone’s camera app, and only the photo you take is passed to the app.
  • Internet: used to load ads.

5. Retention and deletion

Saved places, photos and parking records are removed from the device when you delete them in the app or uninstall the app. They are excluded from device (cloud) backups. The developer keeps no copy.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect children’s personal information.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version and its effective date will be posted on this page. Changes on November 1, 2026: added the auto parking record (Bluetooth, pressure and motion sensors, location for up to 20 minutes after you open the app, notifications) and photos.
